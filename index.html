Firm Philosophy

Investment approach

On God No Capital Investments is organized for the acquisition, underwriting, and ongoing management of a single residential asset. The Firm's approach rests on disciplined tenant selection, consistent collection practices, and direct oversight by its Managing Member. Formal lease documentation has not been executed for the current holding; all terms are established and maintained by direct agreement between the parties, subject to periodic review.
