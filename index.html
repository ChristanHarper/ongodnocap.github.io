Firm Philosophy

Built on a word, not a lease

On God No Capital Investments is a private residential capital group specializing in the acquisition, underwriting, and long-term stewardship of a single, rigorously vetted asset: one (1) room, occupied by one (1) tenant, under one (1) verbal agreement that has never, not once, been written down. We believe in returns. We believe in relationships. Mostly, we believe in getting paid on the first.
